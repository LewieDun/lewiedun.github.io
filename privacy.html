Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Refine is a fitness and nutrition tracking app built by an independent developer. This policy explains what data Refine collects, where it's stored, who it's shared with, and how you can delete it.

In short:

What we collect
Your account details, your height and weight, the workouts you log, your food entries, and your messages to Atlas if you use it.
How we use it
To personalise your plan, show your trends and run Atlas. It is never sold and never used for advertising.
Your controls
Atlas is optional and never changes your data without your confirmation. You can delete your account and its data from Settings at any time.

01Data we collect

  • Account details: the email address and password you sign up with. Email addresses aren't verified, so you don't have to use your real one.
  • Profile: details you enter during setup, such as height, weight and goals.
  • Your logs: workouts, workout templates, food diary entries, meals, supplements, and weight and body measurements.
  • Atlas messages: only if you choose to use the AI assistant. See Third-party services below.
  • Crash reports: anonymous technical data that helps us fix bugs.

02Data stored on your device

Refine is built to work offline. Everything you log is stored in a local database on your phone, and the app works fully without an internet connection.

Camera and scanning

Refine uses your camera to scan barcodes and nutrition labels. Barcode images are processed on your device and never uploaded. When you scan a nutrition label, the photo is sent to our server and our AI provider to read the nutrition values, then discarded. It is not stored.

03Sync

Your data is copied to your Refine account when you create the account, when you log in, and whenever you sync from Settings. Sync lets you restore your data on a new phone or use Refine on more than one device.

Synced data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure (Microsoft Azure, UK region). It is encrypted in transit and at rest. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

04Third-party services

We do not sell your data, and we do not work with advertisers or data brokers. We share data only with the service providers that run Refine:

  • Anthropic (AI provider) processes Atlas messages and nutrition label photos.
  • Sentry (error tracking) receives anonymous crash reports. These contain only technical data. They never include personal information or the content of your Atlas conversations.

Atlas, the AI assistant

Atlas is optional. When you use it, your messages are sent through our server to Anthropic to generate a reply. To answer questions about your training and nutrition, Atlas reads your data on your device and sends only summaries, such as daily totals, trends and personal bests. It never sends your individual food diary entries.

Conversations are not saved, either on your device or on our server. We keep only usage records (time, message count and cost) to enforce limits. Atlas can suggest food diary entries, but nothing is written until you confirm it.

05Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time from Settings in the app. This removes your data from our servers and from your device.
