Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

What We Collect

Refine requires an account. We collect and store:

Account Information

  • Email address (login and recovery)
  • Password (securely hashed)
  • Name, date of birth, gender
  • Height, weight, activity level
  • Fitness goals and nutrition targets
  • Profile picture (optional)

Fitness & Nutrition Data

  • Workouts (exercises, sets, reps, weights)
  • Nutrition logs (foods, calories, macros, meal photos)
  • Body measurements
  • Custom exercises and templates

Camera & Barcode Scanning

We use your camera for barcode scanning to look up nutrition information. Barcode images are processed locally and never uploaded. Meal photos you take are stored as part of your nutrition diary.

Error Tracking

We use Sentry for anonymous crash reports. These contain only technical data, no personal information. Learn more: sentry.io/privacy

How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide Refine's features: storing workouts, syncing across devices, nutrition lookup, and improving stability. We do not use your data for advertising or marketing.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. We do not work with advertisers or data brokers. Only Sentry receives anonymous crash reports.

Data Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text. Your data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure.

Your Rights

Delete your account anytime in Settings → Account. This permanently removes all data from our servers. Or email support@refinemobile.com.

Questions? Email support@refinemobile.com.
