Key Features

Everything you need to refine your fitness journey.

Workout Tracking

Create custom templates, log sets, reps, and weight. Track your progress over time with detailed history.

AI Nutrition Scanning

Snap a photo of any nutrition label and let AI extract the data. Scan barcodes for instant food lookups.

Body Metrics

Track weight, body measurements, and visualize trends with intuitive charts and progress indicators.

Offline-First

All your data lives on your device. No internet required for core features — sync when you want to.

About Refine

Built for people who take their fitness seriously.

Tracking your fitness shouldn't mean juggling five different apps. Refine brings workouts, nutrition, and body metrics into one place so you can focus on what matters — making progress. Everything runs offline on your device, with nothing standing between you and your data.
