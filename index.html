About Refine

Built for people who take their fitness seriously.

Refine is a cross-platform fitness and nutrition tracking app designed to help you build better habits. With customizable workout templates, AI-powered nutrition label scanning, and comprehensive body metric tracking, Refine gives you everything you need in one place. Your data stays on your device with optional cloud sync for backup and cross-device access.
